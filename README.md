# OpenJetty

**Open-source AI apps, finished and free to build on.**

OpenJetty is the open platform for open-source software. Finished AI apps anyone can take, run and build their own product on. MIT licensed, open for good.

Website: [openjetty.ai](https://openjetty.ai)

## Two shelves

Everything on OpenJetty sits on one of two shelves.

| Shelf | What is on it |
|---|---|
| **Products** | Finished apps a business can run. Pick one, set it up, and use it. |
| **Tools** | Building blocks developers build on. The best parts of the apps, cut out so you can use them inside your own. |

## The Jetty Standard

Before I open the source of an app I build, it has to meet all of these.

- **Finished.** It works when you open it. Not a demo, not a starter.
- **Tested by hand.** I run it myself against a written test.
- **Known issues listed.** What is broken is written down, honestly, next to the code.
- **One-command setup.** One command to start it. You bring only your own keys.
- **MIT forever.** Use it, change it, sell what you build on it. That does not change.

The full page is in [STANDARD.md](STANDARD.md).

## Who it is for

Anyone who wants to build with AI.

- **Builders.** Take a finished app, read all of it, and make it yours.
- **Startups.** Start from something that already works, and put your time into what makes you different.
- **Enterprises.** Run it on your own servers, see every line of the code, and change what you need.

## Why I build this in the open

I'm Ajay. I build these apps in the open so that anyone who wants to build with AI has a real starting point: something finished that they can run, read and change.

The apps I build will be MIT licensed. You can take one, build your own product on it, and keep what you build. They stay open for good. A partner project shows its own license.
