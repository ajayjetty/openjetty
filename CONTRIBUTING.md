# How to help

Thank you for being here. OpenJetty is built in the open, and it is better when people say what they need.

## What you can do today

There is no app source in this repo. Each app will have its own repo, with its own guide for code changes. Until an app's source opens, the most useful things are these.

- **Tell me what you need.** Open an issue with the "Tell me what you need" form. Say what you are trying to build or run, in your own words.
- **Report a problem.** If something on [openjetty.ai](https://openjetty.ai), on a live app, or on these pages is wrong or broken, open an issue with the "Report a problem" form.
- **Fix these pages.** If you see a mistake in a document here, open a pull request. Small, clear changes are the easiest to accept.

## A few plain rules

- Write in plain words.
- One subject per issue or pull request.
- Do not put private information in an issue: no passwords, keys, or personal details of other people.
- For a security problem, do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Be kind. See [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
