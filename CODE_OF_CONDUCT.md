# Code of conduct

OpenJetty is for anyone who wants to build with AI. Everyone is welcome here.

## What I ask of everyone

- Be kind and be patient.
- Assume the other person means well.
- Talk about the work, not about the person.
- Say plainly when you disagree, and say why.
- Help people who are new.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, threats, or harassment of any kind.
- Remarks that put people down for who they are.
- Sharing someone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, or using this space to sell something unrelated.

## If something goes wrong

Write to [hello@openjetty.ai](mailto:hello@openjetty.ai). Say what happened and where. I will read it, keep it private, and reply.

If someone breaks these rules, I may remove what they posted, and I may block them from this space.

## Where this applies

In this repo, in its issues and pull requests, and in any other OpenJetty repo that links to this page.
