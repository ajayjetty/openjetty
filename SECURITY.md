# Security

If you find a security problem in anything on OpenJetty, please tell me privately first.

## How to report it

Write to [hello@openjetty.ai](mailto:hello@openjetty.ai) with "Security" in the subject line.

Please do **not** open a public issue for a security problem.

## What to include

- Which app, site or page it is in.
- What you did, step by step.
- What happened, and what you expected to happen.
- Anything that helps me see it for myself.

Please do not include other people's private data in your report. A description is enough.

## What happens next

- I will write back to say I got your report.
- I will look into it and tell you what I found.
- When it is fixed, I will tell you, and I will credit you if you would like that.

## Please

- Give me a fair chance to fix the problem before you tell others about it.
- Do not use the problem to look at, change or delete data that is not yours.

Thank you for helping keep people safe.
